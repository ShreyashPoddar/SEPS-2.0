1. System Introduction & Allocation Rules

The Student Engineering Project System (SEPS) is SRMIST's unified portal designed to govern, automate, and orchestrate final-year capstone engineering allocations across the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering.

The platform enforces strict merit transparency, department stream eligibility, vacancy tracking, and collaborative multi-member team formation.

Core Institutional Policy: Each student is permitted a maximum of two simultaneous applications (Priority 1 and Priority 2). As soon as any faculty guide formally accepts your submission, your team is permanently allocated and all secondary applications are automatically revoked.

2. Two-Step Authentication & Account Setup

SEPS employs a fortified two-step authentication mechanism to eliminate unauthorized access and verify student credentials against institutional registers.

Step 1: Institutional Identifier Verification

Input your official SRM registration number (e.g. RA2311053010076) into the identifier field and submit. The system queries the database to confirm your identity.

Figure 2.1: Login Step 1 – Entering Student Registration Number.

Step 2: Password Authentication

Once verified, the portal displays your full name, department, and role badge. Enter your confidential student password.

Default Student Password Formula:
First 4 lowercase letters of First Name + Last 6 digits of Registration Number
Example: For student SUHAS M (Reg: RA2311053010076), the password is suha010076.
Figure 2.2: Login Step 2 – Confirmed Student Banner and Password Input.

3. Student Profile Verification Gate

Upon first sign-in, students are guided through the Mandatory Profile Gate. You cannot browse or apply for capstone projects until every field is complete and certified.

Figure 3.1: Student Profile portal featuring academic data, portfolio links, and internship declarations.

Key Required Fields:

  • Cumulative GPA (CGPA): Validated numeric value between 0.01 and 10.00 (e.g. 9.35).
  • Department & Section: Pre-assigned by institutional administration (e.g. Section K, ECE Data Science).
  • Portfolio & Resume URLs: Direct links to your LinkedIn profile, GitHub repository, and Google Drive resume.
  • Cohort Track: Select either Regular On-Campus or Corporate Internship (requires company name and duration).
Cohort Mismatch Warning: Student teams must be homogeneous. Regular students cannot team up with Corporate Internship students due to differing review schedules.

8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Why does a project card say "Application Limit Reached"?

Students are limited to two concurrent submissions (Priority 1 and Priority 2). Once both slots are occupied, you cannot apply to further projects unless a guide declines your submission.

Q2: Can students from different branches form a team?

Yes, provided the proposing faculty guide explicitly marked the project's allowed streams as All Streams or included both respective department codes.

Q3: What if I lose my password?

Contact your Section Faculty Advisor. They have administrative privileges to verify your identity and generate a temporary access PIN.
