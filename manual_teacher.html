3. Teacher Dashboard Overview

The Teacher Dashboard serves as the central command console for all supervisory duties, summarizing project proposals, pending applications, and approved groups.

Figure 3.1: Teacher Dashboard displaying metric cards, deadline banner, and active projects.

Key Dashboard Components:

  • Central Capstone Deadline Banner: Displays the institutional cutoff date for student team submissions.
  • Coordinator Controls: Direct access to Set Global Deadline and Statistics Report for designated department convenors.
  • Project Quota Tracker: Real-time counter showing active versus allocated proposals.

4. Proposing & Uploading Capstone Topics

Faculty can propose innovative projects aligned with departmental research interests or funded laboratories.

Figure 4.1: Collapsible Project Upload Form with stream selection and technical domain tags.

Project Proposal Fields:

  • Project Title: Concise, formal academic title.
  • Technical Abstract: Detailed statement outlining problem context, required hardware/tools, and expected outcomes.
  • Allowed Streams: Multiselect filter for department streams (e.g. ECE Core, Cyber Physical Systems, Data Science, All Streams).
  • Domain Category: Approved IEEE disciplines (e.g. AI/ML/DL, Embedded Systems & IoT, Wireless Networks).
  • Sanctioned Team Size: Maximum number of students permitted (typically 1 to 4 members).

5. Reviewing Applications & Team Allocation

When prospective student teams apply, click View Applications on your project card to access the candidate queue.

Figure 5.1: Applications Review portal displaying student CGPA, skills, and proposal pitch.

Candidate Evaluation & Actions:

  • Reviewing Academic Credentials: Inspect member CGPAs, LinkedIn profiles, and cloud resumes.
  • Cohort Track Check: Ensure applicant cohort fits your laboratory mode (Regular On-Campus vs Corporate Internship).
  • Accept Team: Officially locks the team to your project, updates status to Allocated, and notifies members.
  • Decline Application: Rejects proposal, freeing students to seek alternative guides.

9. Frequently Asked Questions & Guide Best Practices

Q1: Can I guide more than two capstone teams?

The department sets a supervisory ceiling of two student groups per faculty member to ensure adequate mentorship quality. Exceptions for industrial collaborations must be approved by the HOD.

Q2: Can I edit project requirements after publishing?

Yes, as long as the project is not yet locked to an accepted team. Once allocated, scope changes must be processed through the formal change ticket system.

Q3: What happens if a team member withdraws mid-semester?

The team leader should raise a Team Reconstitution ticket through the Ticket Tracker for endorsement by the Faculty Advisor and HOD.
